A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

These are four of the heroes who got me into the DC Universe. Each represents something different about what makes these stories worth exploring.

Batman introduced me to the darker, detective-driven side of DC. John Stewart showed me the scale of its cosmic universe and the power of will. Superman represents the hope at the heart of these stories, while Wally West brings the personality and legacy of the Flash.

The Detective Corps is my way of sharing that enthusiasm and helping new readers find their own way into the world of DC Comics.

The Detective Corps is an independent fan-made educational project. It is not affiliated with DC.
